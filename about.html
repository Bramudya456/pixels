About Pixels

Empowering Creators with Free Visual Content

Our Mission

Pixels provides high-quality and completely free stock photos and videos, licensed under the Pixels license. All content is contributed by talented creators around the world.

Why We Exist

Our goal is to help designers, bloggers, marketers, and everyone else who needs visuals by offering them free, beautiful imagery they can use anywhere – no strings attached.

Meet Our Contributors

Pixels thrives thanks to our amazing global community of photographers and videographers. Each contributor helps power creativity by sharing their vision with the world.

Join the Community

Whether you’re looking for visuals or want to share your own work, Pixels is for you. Upload your content, inspire others, and build your portfolio on a platform built for creative freedom.
